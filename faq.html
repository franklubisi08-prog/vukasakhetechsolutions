Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our company, services and project process. If you need more information, contact our team.

What services does Vukasakhe Tech Solutions offer?
We provide professional website development, CCTV camera installation, IT support, software solutions and additional digital and business services.
Where are you located?
We are based along Tonga/Mangweni Road, serving businesses, schools and individuals across the Nkomazi region and surrounding areas.
Do you serve customers outside Nkomazi?
Yes. We can assist customers in other areas depending on the service required and project arrangements.
How much does it cost to develop a website?
Our website development prices depend on the number of pages, features and complexity of the project. We offer affordable packages for individuals, small businesses and organisations.
Will I own my website after payment?
Yes. Once the agreed payment has been completed, the website belongs to the client, subject to the agreed project terms.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Yes. We develop responsive websites that work on smartphones, tablets and computers.
Do you provide domain names and hosting?
We can assist with domain registration, hosting setup and website publishing. Costs and inclusions depend on the selected package.
Do you install CCTV cameras for homes and businesses?
Yes. We install CCTV camera systems for homes, offices, schools, shops and other properties.
Do you offer remote camera viewing?
Yes. Where supported by the camera system and internet connection, customers can monitor their cameras remotely using a smartphone or computer.
Do you supply CCTV equipment?
Yes. We offer CCTV installation packages that include cameras and the necessary recording and installation equipment, depending on the selected package.
What IT support services do you provide?
We assist with computer and laptop troubleshooting, software installation, hardware installation, network cabling, workspace setup and general technical support.
Do you provide on-site IT support?
Yes. On-site support can be arranged depending on your location and the nature of the problem.
How do I request a quotation?
Contact us through WhatsApp, phone or our website enquiry form. Tell us what service you need, and we will assist with a suitable quotation.
Do I have to pay monthly?
No. Our projects are generally paid according to the agreed quotation. Monthly maintenance or additional services are optional where applicable.
How long does it take to complete a project?
Project timelines depend on the type of service, project size, availability of materials and client requirements. We provide estimated completion times before starting.
